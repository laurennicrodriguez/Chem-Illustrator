Using the prototype

Hotkeys work like ChemDraw: point at an atom or bond (it highlights blue) and press a key. Pressing a key that grows something moves the highlight to the new atom, so pressing 1 1 1 draws a chain.

Pointing at an atom

  • o O, n N, s S, f F, l Cl, b Br, i I, p P, h H, c C
  • m Me, e Et, Shift+P Ph, Shift+A Ac, Shift+O OMe, Shift+E CO2Me, Shift+Y Boc, Shift+H Cbz, Shift+F CF3, Shift+N NO2
  • 1 grow a bond, 2 C=O or acetyl, 3/a phenyl, 4 wedge, 5 hashed wedge
  • 6 cyclohexane, 7 cyclopentane, u cyclobutane, v cyclopropane, 8 =CH2, 9 two methyls, z alkyne, Shift+K t-Bu
  • +/- charge, Enter type a label, Delete remove label or atom

Pointing at a bond

  • 1 2 3 single, double, triple
  • w wedge, h hashed wedge (press again to reverse), Shift+H hashed, b bold, d dashed, y wavy
  • l c r move the second line of a double bond
  • a fuse benzene, z fuse cyclopentadiene, 4–8 fuse a ring of that size

Pointing at empty space

  • Space select tool, x bond, Shift+X chain, j benzene, Shift+J cyclopentadiene, t text, e arrow
  • Extra (not in ChemDraw): w wedge tool, h hashed wedge tool, 2 double bond tool, 3–8 ring tools

Everywhere

  • Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, Ctrl+A select all, Delete delete selection
  • Mouse wheel scrolls, Ctrl+wheel zooms, middle or right drag pans, Ctrl+0 fits the drawing
  • Paste a SMILES string (Ctrl+V) to draw it; Ctrl+C copies the selection as SMILES
  • Open a .cdxml file with Open, or drop it on the drawing

Not in this prototype yet

  • Saving, Clean Up, rich-text captions, templates, curved-arrow editing, pictures
