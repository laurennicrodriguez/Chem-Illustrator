Using the prototype

Hotkeys work like ChemDraw: point at an atom or bond (it highlights blue) and press a key. A key that grows something moves the highlight to the new atom, so you can carry on typing (8 then 1 makes an E double bond). 1 and 0 keep the highlight under the mouse, so pressing 1 three times adds three bonds; after a click or another key they move it, so 1 1 1 draws a chain. Every key can be changed.

        Drawing tools

        • Text tool: click to type a caption; the bar above the text has bold, subscript, formula style, Greek letters and symbols. Esc finishes
        • Arrows, shapes and brackets are in the tool bar; drag their handles to change them, and use the Style panel for line width and colour
        • Templates (rings, heterocycles, amino acids, sugars): click empty space to place one, a bond to fuse it on, an atom to join it there
        • Object menu: align, space evenly, flip, rotate, scale and join; or drag the round handle above a selection to turn it. Arrow keys nudge the selection; adds a reaction arrow and a copy of the selection to turn into the product
        • Structure menu: Clean Up Structure evens out bonds and angles while keeping stereochemistry; Fixed Bond Lengths and Fixed Angles can be turned off (or hold Alt while drawing)
        • Structure > Name to Structure () draws a structure from its name: systematic (IUPAC) names, CAS-style names and many common names such as caffeine or ethyl acetate; most drug and trade names are not known. Select a caption holding a name first to replace the caption with the structure. Names are read on this computer by OPSIN, so it works offline. Substituent names such as 4-methoxyphenyl get a wavy-line point of attachment

        Chemistry checks

        • Red boxes mark atoms with too many or too few bonds, labels that can't be read, and atoms sitting on top of others. Point at one to see why; the Warnings panel lists them all, with Ignore. Structure > Check Structure goes to the next one. They are never printed, saved or copied
        • R/S and E/Z are worked out for every structure as you draw. Structure > Show Stereo Labels adds them to the selected structures (or all) as part of the drawing; they update by themselves after edits. Select a stereocentre to hide its label or type your own (for example (R*)). The Show R/S box at the top previews every label in red
        • As in ChemDraw, plain bold bonds count as wedges and plain hashed or dashed bonds as hashed wedges. Where R or S would depend on what an R or Ar group is, no label is given

        Everywhere

        • Mouse wheel scrolls, Ctrl+wheel zooms, middle or right drag pans; the View menu zooms too
        • Copy puts the selection (or the whole drawing) on the clipboard; in the Windows app it pastes into ChemDraw, and into Word and PowerPoint as a picture. Paste takes drawings from ChemDraw, and SMILES or molfile text
        • File menu: New Drawing, New Slides, Open, Save and Save As (.cdxml, .cdx, .pptx), Export PDF, Export Molfile. Each file has a tab; Ctrl+Tab moves between them. You can also drop files on the window

        Not in this prototype yet

        • Rings bonded to a metal through all their atoms (ferrocene); tables (they come with Sheet mode)
